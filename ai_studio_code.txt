I am developing an Application Engineer (AE) Performance Tracker for a calibration lab using Flask, SQLite, Pandas, OpenPyXL, and python-pptx (hosted on PythonAnywhere).

Here is the operational context, business logic, and current project state:

1. KEYSIGHT FISCAL YEAR ALIGNMENT (Nov - Oct):
   - Q1: Nov, Dec, Jan (Note: Nov & Dec of calendar year Y belong to Fiscal Year Y+1, e.g., Nov '25 = Q1 FY'26)
   - Q2: Feb, Mar, Apr
   - Q3: May, Jun, Jul
   - Q4: Aug, Sep, Oct
   - This fiscal calendar is aligned across all database queries, analytics timelines, and quarterly reports.

2. SEGMENT GROUPS & QUARTERLY REPORT (Option B - Proportional Labor Share):
   - The 4 core segment groups are: "CSG", "EiSG+Scope", "STDS LAB", "Legacy+Modular+PMPS (A)+MMA+ Others", plus "MEC Lab" (omitted from reports while its station is inactive).
   - Calibrations and rejections are mapped to segment groups via the `model_groups` table (`Model ID -> Group`). Unmapped models in a quarter trigger an assignment alert/modal on `/analytics` and in upload Pre-Flight.
   - Station-to-group mapping in `/team` serves as the station reference list and fallback for working days if an active AE calibrated zero mapped units in a given month.
   - P.I. is calculated via Option B (Monthly Proportional Labor Share): Each month, an eligible AE's working days are divided proportionally across segment groups based on the share of mapped units they calibrated for each group (`group_days = ae_month_days * (ae_group_units / ae_total_mapped_units)`).
   - Target P.I. is removed from the Quarterly section.
   - Quarterly metrics:
     * Total Volume
     * Avg Monthly Output (Total Volume / 3 for quarters; Total Volume / active months for Full FY)
     * Quarterly P.I. (Group Units / Group Effective Days)
     * QoQ Δ P.I. (Comparison vs previous fiscal quarter; YoY for Full FY)
     * Weighted TAT % (Unified 15/35/35/15 formula)
     * Quality % and Rejections
   - Single Source of Truth: Web UI (`/analytics`), Excel audit export (`/export_quarterly_data`), and Quarterly Slides (`/export_quarterly_pptx`) all use the Option B engine. Full-year PPTX export is disabled on `/analytics`.

3. TAT SLA FORMULA (Unified Everywhere):
   - Weights: CRD_ETE (15%), CRD_NEW (35%), PSC_EXT (35%), PSC_INT (15%).
   - Weighted average of MOS = 1 completion rate. Used identically on Dashboard, History, Analytics, Slides, and Excel.

4. STAFF ROSTER, DASHBOARD & LIFECYCLE RULES:
   - `track_performance = 1` for AEs (calibrating engineers); `0` for Admins/Support (status badge is "Admin", username optional).
   - Engineers require an `activation_date <= month_end` to appear on Dashboard/Analytics/Slides.
   - Resigned members remain included in historical months/quarters up through their resignation month (`resigned_date >= month_start`, or if they calibrated units in that month).
   - Slide 4 (Resource Level Report): Counts ALL staff (AEs + Admins + MECH LAB) unless resigned prior to that month.
   - Transfers: `/team/transfer_member` automatically snapshots the previous station as Initial Station in `engineer_station_history`.
   - Dashboard daily matrix supports double-click inline editing of Hours and Output via `/api/update_cell`, and surfaces missing-attendance warnings and unmapped-technician alerts.
